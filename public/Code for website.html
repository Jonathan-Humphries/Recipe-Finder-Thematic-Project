RECIPE NAME

Serves: 4
Vegan Quick Dinner

Description

A short, delicious summary of what this dish is all about and why you'll love it.

Nutrition Info

  • Calories: 350
  • Protein: 12g
  • Carbs: 45g

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of Flour
  • 2 Large Eggs
  • A pinch of salt
  • 500ml Milk

Steps

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Slowly pour in the milk while stirring constantly.
  3. Heat a pan over medium heat with a knob of butter.
  4. Cook until golden brown on both sides.
