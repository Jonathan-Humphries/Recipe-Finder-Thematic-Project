Spaghetti Aglio e Olio

20 min
Serves 2

A classic Italian pasta dish made with garlic, olive oil, and chilli flakes — simple, bold, and endlessly satisfying. Proof that the best meals need only a handful of great ingredients.

vegetarian vegan pasta italian quick
📋

How to make it

1

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti until al dente according to package instructions. Before draining, reserve 1 cup of starchy pasta water — this is the secret to a silky sauce.

2

While the pasta cooks, peel and thinly slice the garlic cloves. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat until shimmering but not smoking.

3

Add the sliced garlic to the oil and cook gently, stirring often, until golden and fragrant — about 3–4 minutes. Keep the heat low; burnt garlic will make the whole dish bitter.

4

Add the red chilli flakes and cook for 30 seconds more, letting them bloom in the oil and release their heat.

5

Add the drained pasta directly to the skillet along with a generous splash of reserved pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat to coat every strand in the garlicky oil.

6

Remove from heat, add a large handful of chopped fresh parsley, and season generously with salt and black pepper. Toss once more. Serve immediately with grated parmesan if desired.

Chef's Note

The pasta water is the secret — its starchiness helps emulsify the sauce and makes it cling beautifully to the noodles. Don't skip it! Add it a splash at a time until the sauce is glossy and coats the pasta.

Ingredients

Serves 2
spaghetti 200g
garlic cloves 6
extra-virgin olive oil 6 tbsp
red chilli flakes 1tsp
fresh flat-leaf parsley large handful
salt to taste
black pepper to taste
parmesan (optional) to serve
